Access

By Car

Leave the Tomei Expressway at the Gotemba IC and take the R138 in the direction of Lake Yamanaka.
Turn left at the R246 crossing and drive towards Numazu.
Turn right at the Yabai crossing to Komakado Industrial Estate.
Leave the Tomei Expressway at the Susono IC and take the R246 bypass route toward Tokyo.
Turn left at the Yabai crossing to Komakado Industrial Estate.

By Train

・Via JR Tokaido Shinkansen line
Get off at Mishima station. Either take a taxi or change to the JR Tokaido line to go to Numazu Station.
Then change to the JR Gotemba line and get off at Fujioka Station.

・Via JR Tokai, Tokaido line
From Tokyo, change to the JR Gotemba line at Kozu Station and get off at Gotemba or Fujioka Station.
From Shizuoka, change to the JR Gotemba line at Numazu Station and get off at Fujioka Station.

・Via Odakyu line
Get off at Shinmatsuda Station. Walk to the JR Gotemba line at Matsuda Station. Get off the Gotemba line at Gotemba or Fujioka Station. Or take the Resort Super Express Asagiri from Shinjuku Station to travel directly to Gotemba Station.
